# Composer

The message box of a session: a floating card at most 680px wide, with a growing textarea above a row of the agent's controls, ending in Send. One `ComposerDock` serves every session and the new-session draft, and stays at one place in the window, so what you typed survives a switch.

The consumer provides the controls and the send handler. Without a placeholder of its own, the composer reads "Reply to <Agent>". While a permission or question card waits, the app passes "Answer the request above to continue".

- **Dock.** 24px at the sides and 20px under the card in a session. A draft's composer sits inside its column, with no bottom padding.
- **Card.** A column with a 10px gap and 12px padding, on `surface-2` with a `border` edge, `radius-xl` and `shadow-popover`. The edge turns `border-strong` while focused.
- **Text.** The prompt starts two lines tall (44px) in `body` on `fg`, with the placeholder in `fg-subtle`. The note under it is `caption` in `fg-muted`: why Send is blocked. While the agent's options load, skeleton chips say so instead (below).
- **Attachments.** Images show above the textarea as 96×64 thumbnails, with an 18px round remove button inside the corner. Clicking one opens `ImagePreviewDialog`. Other files are name chips.
- **Bottom row, left.** No divider above it; its controls sit 4px apart.
  - Attach is a 28px paperclip button in `fg-muted`.
  - In a running session the agent comes next, as a label: its logo in a 16px tile and its name, 26px tall, not a button, since a session cannot change its agent.
  - Then the agent's controls, in the order model, mode, effort, then the agent's extras (collaboration mode, fast mode and so on).
  - Each control is a 26px chip with `radius-md` (6px), filled `chip`, in `ui-sm` and `fg-secondary`, with no leading icon and a 12px chevron in `fg-muted`. An open chip is filled `surface-hover` with `fg` text.
  - A risky mode's chip ends its name with a 12px `ShieldAlert` in `status-attention`.
- **Overflow.** At most `MAX_INLINE_CONTROLS` (3) controls sit in the row. The rest go into the **More options** ⋯ menu, as one group per control with its choices as radio items. It is a count, not a measurement, so the chips never move while the window is resized.
- **Bottom row, right.** The context use as a 14px ring (a `surface-hover` track and an arc in `fg-muted` as long as the share in use) before the words "38% of context" in `caption` and `fg-subtle`. The words and the arc turn `status-attention` from 80% and `danger` from 95%, and the button opens the numbers behind it. When the agent does not know its window, only the tokens show, with no ring. Then Send.
- **Send.**
  - Send is the only primary control: 28px, `radius-lg`, with an arrow up. It is disabled while the text is empty, and while Send is blocked it is `aria-describedby` the note.
  - `sendLabel` words it ("Start 2 sessions", or "Delegate to Codex" while the message starts with an agent's mention), at 13/500 with a 12px arrow up after the label.
  - In a delegation pair Send waits while the other side works, with the note saying why: "Codex is working on the task you delegated." in A, "Claude Code is working on Codex's report." in B while A answers a report, else "Wait for Claude Code to finish.".
  - While the agent works, Stop takes its place: an outline icon button with an 11px square.
- **Menus.** The mode, model and other chips open `Popover`s with the check on the left and descriptions in `fg-subtle`. In a session they open above the chip, from the bottom of the window. In a draft, whose composer sits near the top of its column, they open below. In both cases a menu flips to the other side when that side has more room, and is capped and scrolls when neither side holds it, so it is never cut off.
  - A model named with a provider tag ("[OpenRouter] kimi-k2") shows its own name on its row, under one heading per provider; search still matches the tag, and the row's tooltip holds the full name.
  - The mode menu is headed "Mode". A risky mode shows its shield after its name, and "Lets the agent act without asking." on its line.
  - A list over 10 choices gets the 28px "Search <label>" field, with the matches in bold and "N of M <label>s" under it in `fg-muted`. While the search has text, the first row is filled `surface-hover`, since Return picks it; the picked choice keeps its check. A match is found in the full name, provider tag and all, so one that runs across the tag ("router] ki") is bold in both the heading and the row; a choice found by its description has the match bold there.
  - A searched menu is 300px wide; one whose choices have a line under them (modes) is 260px; a short plain list is as wide as its words. In these lists only the picked or marked row names its choice in `fg`; the rest read `fg-secondary`.
- **`/` and `@`.** These open lists with the popover's look: above the card in a session, under it in a draft, whose card sits near the top of its column. The prompt keeps focus, and ↑ ↓, Return, Tab and Escape drive the list.
  - "Commands from <Agent>": each command in mono 12 over its description in 11/15 `fg-subtle`.
  - "Delegate to an agent", above the files, only for an `@` at the very start of the message in a session that may delegate (not a draft, a delegated session, a popout or a compare run): one row per agent in use with its 16px logo, its name in `ui-sm` with the match in bold, and its status on the right in `caption`: "Ready" (`fg-muted`), "Not checked" (`fg-subtle`), "Needs sign-in" (`status-attention`, after a dot) or "Not installed" (`fg-subtle`). A row that cannot be picked (needs a sign-in, or not installed) is at 55% opacity and ↑ ↓ skip it. Picking one writes `@<Agent name> ` and leaves the caret after the space; ↑ ↓ run over the agents, then the files.
  - "Files in <folder>": a file icon, the name in mono 12 with the match in bold, and its folder in mono 11 `fg-subtle`.
- **A mention that delegates.** A message that starts with `@<Agent>` and a task hands the task to that agent in a session of its own (see Dialog, "Delegate to another agent"); only the start counts, so "ask @Codex later" is an ordinary message. The session's own agent label, chips and `/` commands stay as they are, and Send reads "Delegate to Codex".
  - The note under the prompt says what the agent starts on, "Codex starts on gpt-5.5-codex · high · Auto review" (a part Zorvo does not know is left out; none known: "Codex starts on its defaults."), then **Change** as a text button in `fg-secondary`, which opens the Delegate dialog with the agent and the task filled in. Delegating from there empties the composer; Cancel leaves it.
  - A reason replaces that line and holds Send: "Write the task after @Codex.", "Codex's commands work in its own session. Delegate the task first, then use them there.", "Codex isn't installed. Install it in Settings → Agents.", "Codex needs a sign-in first.", or "Codex already has a task from this session.".
- **New-session draft.** A left-aligned 720px column (`container-draft`), 64px down, its parts 20px apart: the heading, the agents, what the draft still needs, the card, then the agent's sessions. It is not centred, so nothing jumps when the agent's sessions or a sign-in panel arrive. The heading reads "New session" at 20/28 600 (`-0.02em`); the folder and branch chips sit at its right and wrap under it in a narrow window. A compare run's note ("Each agent starts in its safest mode, in its own worktree.") sits under the card in `caption` `fg-subtle`.
- **Draft chips.** The folder and the branch, beside the heading: the composer's own chip (26px, `radius-md`, filled `chip`, `ui-sm` in `fg-secondary`), 8px apart, with a 12px leading icon and chevron in `fg-muted`. An open chip is filled `surface-hover` in `fg`. Their menus open downward, one at a time with the composer's other menus.
  - **Folder:** search, "Recent folders", **Open another folder…**.
  - **Branch** (in a repository): the branch name in mono 11, and "· new worktree" in `fg-subtle` while that switch is on.
    - Its menu is 360px wide: the search field, then "Local branches" in mono 12 with the check on the left, and "checked out" on the folder's own branch.
    - A line separates the **New worktree** switch, which sits on the left with its label over "Works on its own copy from <branch>. Your checkout stays as it is.".
  - **Agents:** a row under the heading, not a chip. "Agents" in `ui-sm` at 500 `fg-secondary`, the hint "Tick two or more to compare them." in `caption` `fg-subtle` (only in a repository), and **All agents** at the right, a quiet 24px button that opens the list of every agent in use. Under it, up to four tiles in a grid (at least 160px each, 8px apart): the first four agents in use, except that a chosen agent outside them takes the place of the last tile not chosen. A tile is a button, at least 48px tall, `radius-panel`, a `border` edge, with a 22px agent tile, the name in `ui`, and a status line in `caption` (a `status-running` dot for Ready, a `status-attention` dot for Sign in, no dot for Not checked or Not installed). Ticked, it is `aria-pressed`, filled `surface-2` with a `border-strong` edge, its name at 500 in `fg` and a 14px check at the right. A tile whose agent needs an install or a sign-in is never ticked: it shows a chevron, is named "<Agent>, sign in first" or "<Agent>, install first", and pressing it picks that agent alone, which shows what it needs. Outside a repository a press picks the one agent.
    - Its menu (in a repository) is 380px wide, headed "Agents" with "Pick more than one to compare".
    - Each row is a 14px checkbox, filled `fg` when ticked, then the logo, the name and a status: "Ready" (`fg-muted`), "Not checked" (`fg-subtle`), "Needs sign-in" (`status-attention`, after a dot) or "Not installed" (`fg-subtle`).
    - An agent that cannot join is at 55% opacity, and its status describes its checkbox.
    - The footer says "With two or more agents, each runs in its own worktree in its safest mode."
- **While the agent's options load.** A draft asks its agent what it offers. Meanwhile, three skeleton chips (26px, `radius-md`, `chip`, pulsing only with motion allowed, about 72, 96 and 56px wide) sit in the row where the model, mode and effort chips will land, so nothing moves when they arrive. A screen reader hears "Loading <Agent>'s models and commands…" from an `sr-only` status. No line shows under the prompt. This is not on the canvas.
- **Panels that hold a draft back.** The sign-in and install panels sit between the agents and the card, 12px above the card, so the draft reads heading, agents, what is needed, then the message. In a session they sit right above the composer.
- **Compare note.** With two or more agents ticked, "Each agent starts in its safest mode, in its own worktree." sits in the control row after attach, in `caption` and `fg-subtle`. Every other note stays under the prompt.
- **Compare models.** With two or more agents ticked, the row shows one model chip per agent, in the run's order, each led by its 16px logo and named "<Agent> model: <model>". It shows the model the agent will start on: its remembered pick, else its default.
  - An agent whose list Zorvo does not know yet reads "Default". It is asked for its models only when its chip opens. Meanwhile the menu says "Asking <Agent> for its models…" over skeleton rows. If asking fails, it says "Could not ask <Agent> for its models." with **Try again**, and the run still starts on that agent's default. An agent that answers without any model reads "<Agent> does not offer a model choice. It starts on its default."
  - A pick is that agent's remembered model, so it is also its model in its next single-agent draft. An agent that turns out to need a sign-in leaves the run, with a notice.
- **The agent's sessions.** Once asked for, a header reads "Continue a session from <Agent>" (`caption`, 500, `fg-muted`) with "N in this folder" (`fg-subtle`) on the right.
  - Each session is a plain 44px row, `radius-lg`, in `fg-secondary`, with its time in `caption` and `fg-subtle`.
  - The hovered or focused row fills `surface-hover` and shows an outline **Continue** at 26px. Continue is not part of the row's name.
