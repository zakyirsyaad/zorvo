# Message

A message in the chat: yours, or the agent's with its tool runs, and the status of the turn under the last one. Every part of the chat sits in one 680px column (`max-w-chat`), on a canvas padded 28px at the top, 24px at the sides and 16px at the bottom, with 24px between messages.

The consumer provides the message, the agent's name and id (for its logo), and the handlers for the actions that apply.

- **Your message:** a `surface-2` bubble with a `border` edge on the right, at most 80% of the column, radius 12, padding 10px 14px, `body` text in `fg`. No header and no time under it; the time is the bubble's tooltip. Copy and Rewind to here (which asks once more: "Rewind?") show when you point at the message or tab into it.
- **The agent's message:** no bubble. A header with the agent's 22px tile, its name in `ui-sm` weight 500 `fg` and the time in `caption` `fg-subtle` (no time for a message replayed from the agent's history), 10px above the body in `body` 14/22 `fg-secondary`. Once the turn has ended, the whole reply fills `surface-1` on hover (padding 12, radius 10; the padding is there from the start, so nothing moves), and Copy, Fork from here and Read aloud show under it as 24px icon buttons in `fg-secondary`.
- **Tool runs:** no card. A 28px ghost summary as wide as its words, in `ui-sm` `fg-secondary` ("Read 2 files, ran a command"), with "N still running" in `fg-subtle` and "N failed" in `danger` beside it. It opens into the steps on a rail (7px in, a 1px `border` line). Each step is 28px:
  - its kind's icon (a turning `status-running` arc while it runs), the verb in a 44px `fg-muted` column, the path in mono 11px (cut at its start, so the file's name stays in sight; `fg` for an edit) or the command (cut at its end);
  - "New" in `fg-subtle` for an edit that made a file, the lines added and removed in mono, then Done (`fg-subtle`), Failed (`danger`), "Did not finish" (`fg-muted`) or "Running" (`status-running`);
  - a 12px slot at the end that every step keeps, with a chevron on one that opens.
  - A command's output is indented 36px, on `terminal-bg` in `terminal-fg`, with no edge. A diff opens in the same place.
- **Turn status:**
  - While the agent works: a 6px `status-running` dot with a 4px `chip` ring, "Claude Code is working" in `ui` `fg-secondary`, and the time since your message in `caption` `fg-subtle`. Nothing pulses.
  - After a minute of silence: a `surface-2` notice with a clock in `status-attention`, "Claude Code hasn't sent anything for N minutes." as a title in weight 600 `fg` over "It may be stuck." in `fg-muted`, and a 28px **Stop** with a square. Radius 12, padding 12px 12px 12px 16px.
  - A failed turn: one `danger-surface` card (radius 12, padding 12px 12px 12px 16px) with a `danger` edge under the last message, a circle-alert in `danger`, what happened as a title in weight 600 `fg` over the hint or the agent's own words in `fg-muted`, then a 28px **Try again**, Details and Dismiss. Details open under the row.
- **Delegation status:** under A's last message while B works, the turn status's shape: a 6px `status-running` dot with its soft 4px ring, "Codex is working on the task you delegated" in `ui` `fg-secondary`, the time since B's turn started in `caption` `fg-subtle`, then a 24px ghost **Open** (selects B). While B waits on a permission or a question it reads "Codex needs your answer", with a 6px `status-attention` dot and no ring; after a failed turn of B, "Codex's turn failed" with a circle-alert in `danger`. While A itself works, its own turn status shows instead. The line sits in an always-mounted `role="status"` region, and nothing pulses.
  B's own line mirrors it while A works on B's report: "Claude Code is working on Codex's report", the same dot and ring, the time, and **Open** (selects A).
- **Delegated from:** B's one quiet line above its first message, as an imported session says where its earlier messages are: "Delegated from Fix login bug · Claude Code" in `caption` `fg-muted`, with **Open** as a text button. When A is gone it reads "The session that delegated this is gone." with no **Open**; after End delegation, "Delegated from Fix login bug · ended".
- **Report message:** the report is the user's side of the chat, so it is a right-hand bubble (`surface-2`, `border`, radius 12, padding 10px 14px, `body` `fg`). Over it, a header like the agent's: the reporting agent's 16px logo and "From Codex" in `ui-sm` weight 500 `fg-secondary`, right-aligned. The bubble's text is the report as sent.
- **Selection and Reply:** selected text is tinted `selection`. The Reply toolbar is a popover panel (`surface-3`, `radius-panel`, 4px padding) with a 28px **Reply** in `fg`.
- **The folder card is gone:** the sidebar and the top bar say where a session runs. An imported session whose history stays in the agent says so in one quiet line above its first message.
